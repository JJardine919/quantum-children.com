Quantum Children is built on an entropy process-intelligence engine that measures how "ordered" or "chaotic" a sensor stream is, moment to moment. Healthy equipment has a fingerprint. When that fingerprint shifts — a sensor drifting out of calibration, a process sliding toward a fault — the engine sees it in the numbers before it shows up as a failure.
